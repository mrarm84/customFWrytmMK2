## What it does and how

A new PERF mode (performance locks are gone with 0024's cut). In PERF, a plain
pad press (no FUNC) edits the sound of the pad's track, pad 1 = T1 .. pad 12 =
T12, with the same fixed recipe each press, so presses stack:

- FILTER FRQ down 5% (6.4 knob steps), FILTER RES up 5%;
- AMP DEL and REV sends up 5%;
- LFO at random, every knob in the first quarter of its range, near 0: SPD
  1..16, MUL one of the six slowest, WAV any of the seven, MOD FRE or TRG, SPH
  0..31, FAD 0..16, DEP +1..32, DST one of 0004's sixteen destinations (the 8
  machine parameters, sample FIN/STA, filter FRQ/RES, amp DEC/PAN, DEL/REV).

The edits go through the sound set's own delta handler (param_set_apply_delta,
as a knob turn) and param_set_value for DST, so they show on the pages, mark
the kit edited and are saved with it; recalling the kit (RELOAD) undoes them.
FUNC + pad still opens stock's PERF edit view. All twelve pads are lit orange
in PERF ({15, 5, 0} on the 0..15 scale stock's palette uses).

Hooks: PerformanceView's key handler entry (0x400ce112, a detour) and its
vtable slot 5, the LED pass (0x401e2428, a patched pointer).
