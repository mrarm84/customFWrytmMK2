## What it does and how

CTRL IN: fixed, no settings. A gate into CTRL IN 1 plays T1 (pad 1, BD), into CTRL IN 2
T2 (pad 2, SD), in any CONTROL INPUT mode, OFF included (2026-11-13: a version
that left ports in mode OFF alone played nothing on the unit, whose ports were
OFF). Both ports' thresholds come from CTRL IN 1's millivolt conversion: with
CTRL IN 2's own, a gate into it never played its track on the unit.

AUDIO IN (2026-11-14): SETTINGS > AUDIO ROUTING > AUDIO IN, device-wide, saved
with the device's settings (gap 0x405c8ea0, tag AINT): SAMPLE (default) leaves
the inputs alone; TRIG plays T11 from AUDIO IN L and T12 from AUDIO IN R on a
positive spike above ain_on % of full scale, re-armed below ain_off %; TRIG INV
the same on negative spikes. The inputs are AC-coupled: a gate arrives as a
spike at each edge, its own polarity at the start and the other at the end, so
one of TRIG / TRIG INV plays at the gate's start and the other at its end. The
input still sounds through the mixer at TRIG: turn its level down there. Read
from the receive buffer (words 10, 11, every 4th frame), so the block before:
0.67 ms later than CTRL IN. With 0023's meter built in, word 10 is the meter's,
so AUDIO IN L at TRIG plays nonsense then.

All ports: note 60 (the track's own pitch) at velocity 127, once: one gate is
one trig. The note-off follows len_ms later whatever the gate's length; falling
below the off level only re-arms the port, and for 2 ms after a trig the port is
not read, so a bouncing edge cannot trig twice. At power-up each port must fall
below its off level before its first trig and is not read for 170 ms. CTRL IN
thresholds are turned into each port's raw units by stock's millivolt conversion
(0x4013f3fa), as its CV levels are. Stock's CONTROL IN MOD routes keep working on
the same port.

The edges are found in the audio interrupt just after adc_pedal_update
(0x40120e9e, every 0.67 ms) on the raw converter result, not the smoothed value,
which lags by tens of milliseconds. Each edge signals the MIDI task's semaphore;
the task plays it at the top of its loop (0x40082d12) through stock's note_on and
note_off, as Overbridge's notes do (source 2), so the engine's note lists are only
touched from the task that already touches them for MIDI.

## Build parameters

Set in `mod.toml` under `[params]`:

- `len_ms` = `50`
- `ain_on` = `12`
- `ain_off` = `4`
