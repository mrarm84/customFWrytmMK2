## What it does and how

PHS, a phaser, on the REVERB page's encoder G (was SRR): FX word 29, which
stock's kit save and load carry but no record names; record 460 (hidden CB TEST's
CB_VCA) in the reverb records' shape, so it is saved with the kit, p-lockable and
on the FX LFO. The knob is the rate, 0 off, 0.03..8 Hz; four allpasses on the
mono sum at 24 kHz, corner swept 200 Hz..4.5 kHz, feedback 0.7, half dry and half
wet. The press cycles OFF (label PH-), on the reverb's output (PHS), on the
delay's input (PHS.); device-wide (cfg byte 8).

DIST FILTER on the DISTORTION page's empty encoders D, E, F: FRQ, RES, TYP. A
biquad (0013's RBJ sections and tables) over the digital PRE signal that feeds
the analog distortion and compressor - with DAC SUM on, every voice and the PRE
returns. TYP OFF, LP2, BP, HP2, BS, popped up with stock TYP's names; FRQ
stock's semitone scale; RES the Q. FX words 38..40, which the engine uploads
with locks and LFO but stock's kit save does not carry: kept in the stored FX
block's unused +0x50..+0x53 with a tag. Records 459, 456, 458 (hidden CB TEST
and CY TEST machines'). SETTINGS > AUDIO ROUTING > DIST FILTER turns it off
device-wide.

REVERB 2 (page 14, the REVERB view's page vector grown to {9, 14}; 0013's
page_info answers it): OCT, SRR, HIS, CHR, DEP, CMB, CMK, RNG. BIT (record 446)
is removed: HH_VCA's record never turned as a plain knob; 446 is stock again.
- All eight are records 341 and 334..340 (BD HARD 2's, kind REVERB, words 49 and
  42..48, modflags 0), not FX words (all 42 are taken), so not lockable;
  FxParameterSet's get (0x401dcc18) and write (0x401dcc20) go to fxs_get/fxs_put,
  which keep them in rv2_val. The kit's FX save writes seven, tagged 0xaa, to the
  stored block's +0x64..+0x6b (zeroed by that save, never read by its load); OCT
  goes in DIST TYP's stored byte bits 3..7 and FRQ's and RES's bit 7.
- Each knob's press moves its effect between the reverb's output and the delay's
  input (a dot on the label marks the delay): SRR its own srr_mode (cfg byte 6;
  FUNC + hold the momentary ALL), CHR and DEP the chorus (cfg byte 7, 1 REV, 2
  DLY), HIS, CMB, CMK, RNG, OCT a bit each in cfg byte 9.
- CHR the chorus rate (0 off), DEP its sweep (to 16 ms around 7 ms), 60 % wet.
- rv2_go, once on the reverb's output and once on the delay's input, each with
  its share of cfg byte 9: CMB a comb (512 * 2^(-CMB/16) pairs at 24 kHz; CMK its
  feedback to 0.93), RNG a ring modulator (20 Hz..4.8 kHz), OCT an octave down
  (the 480 Hz-lowpassed mono sum times a flip-flop turned at every other zero
  crossing), HIS hiss to -42 dBFS; at 16 bits while any is on there. Each costs
  nothing at 0.
The code is in cave31 (BD FM 2's routine; machine 41 points at DISABLE) and the
encoder-press handler in cave8 (freed by 0012), the chorus in cave10. The chorus and comb lines are in
the SDRAM 0022 frees (0x4B700000..0x4B7047FF).

The passes run on the audio interrupt's main path; the alternate path taken
while 0x8000e578 is set (0x4011fd9c) renders the reverb SRR, the filter and the
momentary unprocessed (the DLY SRR, inside the effects routine, runs on both).
